MISSION

Tracking the Semiconductor Arms Race

The global competition for semiconductor supremacy is reshaping international relations, economic policy, and technological innovation. This terminal aggregates real-time intelligence on chip technology, export controls, and the evolving tech rivalry between the United States and China.

Built as a tool for monitoring one of the most consequential technology stories of our time—where silicon has become as strategic as oil, and manufacturing capability translates directly to economic and military power.

DATA SOURCES

How It Works

This terminal continuously aggregates news from trusted sources via NewsAPI, focusing on four key areas:

  • Semiconductors & US-China Relations: Trade policy, export restrictions, and diplomatic tensions
  • Chip Technology Geopolitics: Manufacturing capacity, supply chain security, and technological innovation
  • TSMC, China & Taiwan: The critical role of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company in global chip production
  • Export Controls: US restrictions on semiconductor equipment, technology transfer, and strategic competition

Articles are automatically deduplicated, filtered by relevance, and updated in real-time. The terminal pulls from major financial publications, technology news outlets, and policy journals.
